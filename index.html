What is an Electrostatic Precipitator?

An Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP) is an air-cleaning technology that uses an electrostatic field to charge airborne particles and collect them on collector plates.

For commercial kitchen exhaust, ESP technology can be used to control smoke, grease, oil mist and fine particulate matter.

How AIRoTAKE ESP Works

01

Dirty Air Inlet

Kitchen exhaust containing smoke, grease and oil mist enters the filtration system.

02

Pre Filter

Large particles and grease droplets are intercepted before the electrostatic stage.

03

Ionizer Section

High voltage creates an electrostatic field that charges airborne particles.

04

Collector Cells

Charged particles are attracted to grounded collector plates and captured.

05

Clean Air Outlet

Cleaner air continues downstream through the exhaust system.
